Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Trivia Rumble Elite collects when you play via Discord Activities or a web browser, how that information is used, and how you can request removal of your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Discord Profile Information (when playing in Discord): With your one-time OAuth authorization (identify scope), the Game receives your Discord user ID, username/display name, and avatar so your profile and score appear accurately on the leaderboard.
  • Browser Guest Identifier (when playing on the web): If you play outside Discord, the Game generates a random guest identifier stored in your browser's local storage (localStorage) to preserve your score between visits.
  • Gameplay & Leaderboard Data: Selected answers, answer timestamps, accumulated points, online status, and last-seen timestamps are stored to synchronize live rounds and rank players.
  • Standard Edge Request Metadata: Requests pass through Cloudflare Workers to synchronize round clocks and complete Discord OAuth token exchanges. OAuth access tokens are returned to your active session and are not stored in our database.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Trivia Rumble Elite does not read Discord server messages, private DMs, voice audio, friend lists, email addresses from Discord, or financial information.

3. How We Use Information

  • To synchronize trivia questions and countdown timers across all concurrent players.
  • To calculate round speed bonuses and maintain global and live player leaderboards.
  • To remember returning players' scores and display names across sessions.
  • To protect the Service against automated abuse or score tampering.

4. Data Storage & Third-Party Services

Gameplay state, player scores, and round answers are stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database accessed exclusively through authenticated server-side requests. Application hosting and edge routing run on Cloudflare Workers, and Discord Activity authentication runs through Discord's Embedded App SDK and OAuth2 API.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertisers.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

Player profiles and leaderboard scores are retained while the Service operates so returning players keep their progress. Browser guests can clear their local guest ID at any time by clearing site data in their browser.

If you played via Discord or browser and would like your stored player record or score deleted from the database, email walusimbileon2@gmail.com with your player ID or Discord username and your request will be processed promptly.

6. Children's Privacy

Trivia Rumble Elite is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable minimum digital consent age in your country). If you believe data from an underage user has been stored unintentionally, please contact us for immediate removal.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Game evolves. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
